Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TikTokDown - Video Organizer ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you authenticate via TikTok.

1. Information We Collect

When you sign in via TikTok Login Kit, we only access user data explicitly authorized by you through standard OAuth scopes:

  • Profile Information (user.info.basic): Display name, public avatar URL, and open ID for session authentication.
  • Video Metadata (video.list): Titles, creation timestamps, and public links of your published content to display within your dashboard.

2. How We Use Information

We use the retrieved information exclusively to:

  • Authenticate your session.
  • Render your personal media dashboard and sorting tools.

We do not sell, rent, or distribute personal data to third parties or advertising networks.

3. Data Storage and Retention

Session tokens and temporary identifiers are stored securely and discarded upon user sign-out or token expiry. We do not maintain persistent copies of private user communications or sensitive financial data.

4. Data Deletion and Revocation

You can revoke access to this application at any time directly through your TikTok account settings (Settings > Security > Manage app permissions). To request complete erasure of local session logs, contact juliogilca@gmail.com.
